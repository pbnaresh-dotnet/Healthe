# Finance Policy v1.2.7 — Outlet Confirmation Discount Correction

**Effective:** 2026-10-08  
**Source reference:** `FIN-Phase-2D-OUTLET-CONFIRMATION-DISCOUNT-CORRECTION`

## Rule

When an outlet reviews or confirms a customer package, the original subscription discount is already stored as a **currency amount** in `Subscription.SubscriptionDiscountAmount`.

That amount is the base discount for confirmation calculations.

The stored `OutletDiscountValue` is an input value whose meaning depends on `OutletPackageDiscountType`:
- `Percent`: percentage of gross meal amount;
- `Fixed`: currency amount;
- `None`: zero.

A percentage value must never be subtracted from a currency discount amount.

## Calculation

1. Reconstruct gross meal amount from scheduled non-cancelled meals.
2. Use the persisted subscription discount amount as the base discount.
3. Calculate any new outlet-confirmation discount according to its validated type.
4. Cap total discount at gross meal amount.
5. If a final meal amount is explicitly supplied, derive the resulting total discount from gross minus that final amount.
6. Recalculate restaurant tax, platform service fee, commission and outlet settlement from the resulting discounted meal amount.

## Audit

The finance calculation snapshot records the confirmation discount type, requested value, calculated amount, final meal amount, additional outlet discount and reason.

Historical snapshots are immutable and remain tied to the finance policy version effective when the calculation was performed.
