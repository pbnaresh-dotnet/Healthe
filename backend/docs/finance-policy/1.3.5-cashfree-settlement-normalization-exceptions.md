# Finance Policy v1.3.5 — Cashfree Settlement Normalization & Reconciliation Exceptions

**Effective:** 2026-10-08 UTC  
**Source code reference:** `FIN-Phase-2E-CASHFREE-SETTLEMENT-NORMALIZATION-EXCEPTIONS`

## Purpose

Define how Cashfree settlement reports are normalized into the provider-neutral settlement accounting model and how unmatched or financially inconsistent rows are handled.

## Rules

1. Cashfree report fields are normalized through aliases for payment ID, settlement ID, transaction/gross amount, service charge/fee, service tax/fee tax, settlement/net amount, settlement date, UTR/bank reference and adjustment fields.
2. A settlement row must contain a stable provider payment ID and settlement ID. Amount-only matching is prohibited.
3. The matched internal payment must be `Paid`.
4. Provider-reported gross amount is compared with the immutable paid transaction amount when supplied.
5. Net settlement is independently calculated as:
   `paid amount - gateway fee - gateway fee tax + provider adjustment`.
6. When the provider supplies a net settlement amount, it must agree with the independent calculation after two-decimal rounding.
7. Missing payment records, non-paid matches, malformed financial values, gross mismatches and net mismatches are persistent reconciliation exceptions.
8. Exceptions retain the normalized source row JSON, provider identifiers, exception type and error message. They are not silently reconciled.
9. Duplicate settlement records remain idempotent; an existing settlement is reused.
10. Exception resolution is an operational action and must retain resolver and resolution notes.

## Audit evidence

The system retains the provider payment ID, settlement ID, raw normalized row, calculated amounts, reported amounts when available, reconciliation status and resolution metadata.

## Accounting boundary

Gateway fees, fee taxes and provider adjustments remain separate from the customer-facing HealthApp platform service fee. Provider settlement data describes payment custody/settlement and does not redefine revenue ownership or tax liability.
