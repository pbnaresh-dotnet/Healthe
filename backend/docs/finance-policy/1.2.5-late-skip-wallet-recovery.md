# Finance Policy v1.2.5 — Configurable Late-Skip Fee & Deferred Recovery

**Effective:** 2026-10-08  
**Source reference:** `FIN-Phase-2D-LATE-SKIP-WALLET-RECOVERY`

## Rule

Each outlet configures its own late-skip fee. The production default is **₹50.00**. The fee is charged when a customer skips a scheduled meal at/after the configured cutoff.

## Wallet and receivable treatment

1. A late skip creates a debit in the customer's wallet ledger.
2. If the wallet remains positive/zero after the debit, the charge is treated as covered by the customer's existing wallet funds.
3. If the wallet cannot cover the debit, the customer's late-skip receivable remains outstanding.
4. The outstanding late-skip amount is added to the next package payment.
5. The recovery amount is kept separate from the new package's outlet sale amount and does not increase the package's meal, delivery, tax or commission calculation.

## Settlement and audit

The recovery is represented by a separate `LateSkipFeeRecovery` platform transaction linked to the later package, while the original late-skip debit retains the original subscription and meal-selection references. This allows settlement reporting to attribute the recovered amount back to the original outlet/package without treating it as a new meal sale.

Changing the outlet's late-skip fee affects future skips only. Existing late-skip charges retain their original amount.
